DOST AI – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 6, 2025
Last Updated: December 6, 2025

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how mynomadshop (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information for our mobile application DOST AI, a generative AI chatbot for innovation, learning, and creation.

A. Information We Collect

1. Information You Provide Directly

Optional data used locally to personalize the chatbot:

  • Name
  • Profession
  • Interest

2. Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Features accessed, session duration, and performance metrics.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID with user consent).
  • Log Data: IP address and text prompts (“Input”), sent to the third-party AI provider to generate responses (“Output”).
  • Analytics Data: Collected through analytics partners such as Firebase.

B. Chat Data Handling

mynomadshop does NOT collect or store chat history on our servers.

  • Processing: Your Input is sent only to the AI model provider to generate Output.
  • Local Storage: Chat history is stored only on your device. You control its deletion.

C. How We Use Your Information

  • Personalization: Tailoring chatbot behavior using your locally stored preferences.
  • App Functionality: Sending Input to the AI model provider to generate Output.
  • Security & Stability: Monitoring device and usage data.
  • Analytics: Understanding app performance and trends.

D. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Developer Retention: No chat logs or personal data are stored. Only anonymized usage data and temporary IP logs for security.
  • Chat History Deletion: Deleting the chat or uninstalling the app removes all stored data.

E. Data Sharing

  • AI Model Provider: Input is shared only for generating responses.
  • Service Providers: Usage & device data shared with analytics/crash services.

F. Children's Privacy

DOST AI is intended for users aged 13 and above.
